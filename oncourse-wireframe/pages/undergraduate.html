How OnCourse Stages the High School Journey

Early start removes final-year stress. We break admissions preparation into logical developmental stages:

Stage 01 Class 8 & 9

Curiosity & Academic Breadth

Exploration without premature pressure. Diagnostic personality assessments, reading programs, subject choice optimization, and establishing foundational study habits.

  • • Subject selection advisory (IB / IGCSE / CBSE)
  • • Broad extracurricular discovery
  • • Critical thinking & communication mentorship
Goal: Unlocking Intrinsic Passions
Stage 02 Class 10 & 11

The Extracurricular Spike & Leadership

Moving from broad participation to deep, distinctive impact. Independent research papers, social initiatives, Olympiads, and standardized test planning (Digital SAT/ACT).

  • • Mentored independent capstone projects
  • • Standardized testing diagnostic timeline
  • • Summer school & research lab placement advisory
Goal: Developing a Distinctive Spike
Stage 03 Class 12

Strategic Narrative & Application Execution

Translating four years of authentic achievements into compelling essays, activity sheets, recommendation positioning, and interview mastery for ED/EA and Regular Decision.

  • • Common App, UCAS & UC narrative sculpting
  • • High-touch essay brainstorm & review rounds
  • • Mock alumni & collegiate interviews
Goal: Flawless Application Execution
